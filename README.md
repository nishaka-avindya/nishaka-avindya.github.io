# Nishaka Avindya

I'm Nishaka Avindya, an SEO Specialist based in Sri Lanka. I work across technical SEO, on-page optimization, off-page link acquisition, and search visibility in AI answer engines — and I build SEO automation tools in Google Apps Script.

I've worked in search since 2024, and I'm currently Search Engine Optimization Specialist at Trident Media Works.

## What I work on

**Technical SEO.** Finding and fixing the crawl, indexing, and rendering issues that stop pages from being discovered or served. Site architecture, Core Web Vitals, and page speed, coordinated with dev teams.

**On-page SEO.** Meta titles and descriptions, heading structure, URL design, internal linking, and structured data — optimized for traditional search engines, AI-powered search surfaces, and human readers at the same time.

**Off-page SEO and link acquisition.** Backlink profile analysis, disavowing harmful links, prospecting, and outreach.

**Keyword research and content SEO.** Keyword research, SERP analysis, competitor research, content audits, and search intent mapping.

**Entity and AI search visibility.** Auditing how brands are described and cited across Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Grok, and Claude — then closing the gap against competitors.

## How I work

I build SEO tooling in Google Apps Script so that repetitive audits and reporting run themselves rather than eating a day each month. Crawl data, entity monitoring, and keyword intent mapping all live in Google Sheets, scripted end to end.

**Tools I work with:** Google Search Console · Google Analytics · Semrush · Ahrefs · Screaming Frog SEO Spider · Google Apps Script · HTML & CSS

## Background

I hold a Bachelor of Management Studies (Honours) from The Open University of Sri Lanka. Alongside SEO, I'm expanding into paid media — Google Ads, Meta Ads, YouTube Ads, and LinkedIn Ads — with the goal of working across both organic and paid search.
